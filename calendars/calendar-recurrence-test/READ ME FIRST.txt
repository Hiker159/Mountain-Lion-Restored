RECURRING CALENDAR ALERT TEST — PREVIEW 01 OR 02

Purpose: test exceptions to recurring events while restored Calendar is closed.
This adds a diagnostic command only. It does not change apps, data, login
settings or power settings. Allow about 20 minutes, keeping the laptop awake.

SETUP
1. Copy Test Recurring Alerts.command into the Calendar folder of your existing
   restored-apps package. Keep the apps at their current location.
2. Open restored Calendar using its Open Calendar.command. Confirm your local
   calendar is visible. Use a disposable local calendar, not Contacts Birthdays.
3. Run Test Recurring Alerts.command and leave its Terminal window open.

CREATE THREE SEPARATE DAILY REPEATING SERIES
Choose times at least 10 minutes ahead so you have time to finish all edits.
Use timed events today (not all-day), repeat every day, and set one Message
with Sound alert at the time of the event. Use short disposable titles:

  Repeat Control: today at T
  Repeat Moved:   today at T + 2 minutes
  Repeat Deleted: today at T + 4 minutes

For example, if it is 3:00, use 3:10, 3:12 and 3:14.
Before changing anything, confirm that each series also appears tomorrow.

EDIT ONLY TODAY'S OCCURRENCE
Move Repeat Moved from T+2 to T+6 (3:12 to 3:16 in the example). When Calendar
asks, choose Only This Event, not all/future events. Delete today's Repeat
Deleted occurrence and again choose Only This Event. Leave Repeat Control alone.
Check tomorrow: all three original occurrences should still exist at their
original times. Today's moved event should appear only at its new time.
If Calendar's choice differs or an edit changes the whole series, stop and
report it rather than proceeding with an ambiguous setup.

OBSERVE WITH CALENDAR CLOSED
Quit Calendar using Command-Q before T. Do not stop its alert helper.
Keep the laptop awake and do not change its clock. Record exact alert times
and whether sound plays. Close notifications normally; do not snooze them.

Expected:
  T:   one Repeat Control alert with sound.
  T+2: no Repeat Moved alert at its original time.
  T+4: no Repeat Deleted alert.
  T+6: one Repeat Moved alert with sound.

After T+8, reopen Calendar with Open Calendar.command. Confirm today's exception
edits persisted and tomorrow's three occurrences remain unchanged. This checks
future event presence, not delivery of tomorrow's alerts; that remains a later
longer-duration test.

COLLECT
Return to the waiting Terminal window, press Return and answer the five short
questions. The command collects helper status/logs and available system/power
messages without restarting the helper. Review and send Calendar-recurrence ZIP.
Logs are supporting evidence; observed delivery and sound are essential.

CLEANUP
After collecting, delete the three test series (including their future events)
so you do not receive daily test alerts. If you used a disposable calendar
containing only these events, you may delete that calendar instead.
